Hello! I am a predoctoral researcher in astrophysics. I work at the boundary between cosmology and big data analysis, with a touch of high-performance software development. My work consists of translating physical problems into robust software solutions.

A large part of my research is focused on the extraction of irregular structures (also known as dark matter halos) from cosmological simulation data. I firmly believe that the discoveries in cosmology depend on our capacity to interpret results; to achieve that, we need precise predictions. My philosophy on software development has two main pillars:
 - **Efficiency:** I want my code to work, but I also want it to be fast. Given the large volume of data incoming, we need scalable solutions.
 - **Open Source:** I firmly believe that the code revised by thousands of people is bound to be better than the code revised by just five. Additionally, it is more likely to contribute to society meaningfully.

My main research lines include:
- Large-scale structure formation
- Disruption processess of satellite galaxies in Milky Way-like systems
